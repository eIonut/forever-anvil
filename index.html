      A new race for both factions: High Order Skyborne (Alliance) can be Mages, Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) can be Shamans.

      Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, Undead Paladin, Human Hunter, Gnome Priest and Dwarf Shaman.

      Choose original visuals or HD models, quest markers, one-bag and transmog. Switch any time.

      Two talent setups for 50 gold from your trainer, free to swap outside combat.

      A new 15v15 battleground off Dustwallow Marsh, plus season-long PvP goals.
