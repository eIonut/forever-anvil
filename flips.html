Good to know

  • Prices are a snapshot taken about once an hour — other players spot flips too, so they can be gone by the time you look.
  • "Units below vendor price" counts only the listings cheap enough to flip; buying more than that won't be profitable.
  • Only listings with a buyout are counted. A flip must earn at least 10 copper and 5% of the vendor price per item — tiny margins on expensive items aren't worth the risk.
  • Check the vendor price in the item's tooltip in-game before buying in bulk.
