How to read this

  • Market price is the average of the cheapest 10% of units on sale. It's steadier than the lowest price, which one bargain listing can drag down.
  • Sell where? compares the market price after the Auction House's 5% cut with what a vendor pays. It ignores the listing deposit and whether the item actually sells.
  • Prices refresh about once an hour, and history covers up to 30 days.
