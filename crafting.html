How it works

  • Materials cost uses each material's market price on the Auction House you picked. If a material isn't on the AH (like threads and dyes sold by trade vendors), we use Blizzard's vendor price — open "Materials" to see which.
  • Profit = AH price of what you make, minus the AH's 5% cut, minus materials. It doesn't include listing deposits, whether it actually sells, or the skill-ups you gain.
  • "?" means at least one material has no price, so we can't work out a profit.
  • Recipe data comes from Blizzard and is refreshed every 25 days; prices every hour.
