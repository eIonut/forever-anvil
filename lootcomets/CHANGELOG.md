## 1.0

- **Loot Comets**: every item you loot flies out of the corpse (or the loot window) and into the exact bag slot it lands in, in a glow of its quality colour, and the slot flashes as it arrives. With your bags closed, it flies into that bag's button on the bag bar. Works with the game's bags and with Bagnon.
- Made for WoW: Forever, and listed for Retail and the Classic versions too (Classic Era, Burning Crusade, Wrath, Cataclysm and MoP Classic).
- Rare items land with sparkles; epic items with a bigger burst, a sound and a glow around the edges of the screen.
- More of something you already carry gets a small, quick comet and a "+3" over its slot.
- Items from vendors, quests, mail, trades and crafting fly too, out of the window they came from. Moving items around your bags, swapping gear and the bank are left alone.
- **Make it yours** (Esc → Options → AddOns → Loot Comets, or `/comets`):
  - **Comet**: a round icon, a square icon with a coloured border, or a glowing orb.
  - **Trail**: dots, a smooth streak, sparkle dust that drifts down, or none.
  - **Colour**: the item's quality, one colour you pick, or rainbow.
  - **Arc**: flat, normal or a high loop.
  - **Landing**: subtle, normal or big; sparkles and the epic screen glow can be turned off.
  - **Speed and size** sliders, grey items, the small stack comets, the counts and the sound.
  - Every change launches test comets so you see it at once; "Reset the look" puts it back. `/comets test` launches one comet of each quality any time.
