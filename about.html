About the Tome

A fan-made hub for World of Warcraft: Forever.

What this site is

Forever Tome gathers the most useful WoW: Forever information from around the web — official hotfixes, community guides, databases and tools — and adds tools of its own, like the Dungeon Finder and the journey checklist.

News and guide entries are short summaries in our own words, and every one links to the original creator. For the full guide, read it there: those writers did the hard work.
