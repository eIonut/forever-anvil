About the Tome

Fan-made tools for World of Warcraft: Forever.

What this site is

Forever Tome is a small set of free tools for WoW: Forever: auction house prices, vendor flips, crafting profits and a crafting planner, a talent calculator and a character lookup.

Prices, recipes, talents and character profiles come from the official Blizzard API and are refreshed automatically.
