## 1.0

- **Landfall**: arriving somewhere shows a title card in place of the game's plain zone text: the name with an ornamental divider opening under it, then the area you're in, whose territory it is and its level range against yours.
- **Territory**: Horde, Alliance, contested or a sanctuary, in its colour, with the faction's crest. When you're flagged for PvP in contested or enemy land, the card says you can be attacked.
- **Level range**: every zone's and dungeon's levels (Forever's new zones included), and how they suit you: dangerous, a little high, good for your level, or below you.
- **Better zones for your level**: when a zone is far above or below you, the card names two that suit you, on your own continent first and never the other faction's land.
- **Danger warning**: arriving somewhere 5 or more levels above you flashes the edges of the screen red, with war drums.
- **Dungeons here**: the dungeons and raids whose entrance is in the zone, each coloured for how it suits you.
- **Inns**: "Resting", whether it's your hearthstone's inn, and how long until you're fully rested.
- **First visits and discoveries**: a "First visit" stamp on a zone you've never explored, and "Discovered" for each new area as you find it.
- **Areas inside a zone**: a smaller card for villages, camps and other areas. Riding back and forth over a border doesn't repeat them, capital cities stay quiet, and flight paths only show new zones (each can be switched on).
- **Dungeons and raids**: the name, the kind, difficulty and size, the level range and your first visit.
- **Four styles**: Classic (Morpheus lettering and a gold divider), Bronze (on a bronze plaque, like Forever's own windows), Cinematic (thin black bars slide in behind your interface, spaced ivory capitals in the lower third) and Minimal (one small line). Short, normal or long; small, normal or large; drag it wherever you like.
- **Sounds you pick**: a sound for a new zone (swell, horn call, bells or harp), a discovery (chime, sparkle or bells) and danger (war drums or horn call), each with a play button, or none.
- **Minimap button**: click for a quick menu (a card for here, style, length, size, the arrival sound, switches), right-click for the settings, drag to move it.
- A card when you log in. Settings: Esc → Options → AddOns → Landfall, or `/landfall`. `/landfall test` shows a card for where you are.
