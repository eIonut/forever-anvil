## Description

```markdown
# Landfall

**Arriving somewhere should feel like it.** Landfall replaces the game's plain zone text with a title card: the name in fine lettering with a gold divider opening beneath it, and everything you'd want to know as you cross the border.

## What a card tells you

- **Whose land it is**: Horde, Alliance, contested or a sanctuary, in its colour, with the faction's crest.
- **PvP warning**: when you're flagged in contested or enemy territory, the card says you can be attacked.
- **Level range for you**: every zone's and dungeon's levels, including Forever's new zones, and how they suit you: dangerous, a little high, good for your level, or below you.
- **Better zones for your level**: too high or too low here? The card names two zones that suit you, on your continent first, never the other faction's.
- **Danger warning**: walk somewhere 5+ levels above you and the screen's edges flash red to war drums.
- **Dungeons here**: every dungeon and raid entrance in the zone, coloured for your level.
- **First visits and discoveries**: a "First visit" stamp on a zone you've never explored, and "Discovered" as you find each new area.
- **Dungeons and raids**: kind, difficulty, size and level range.
- **Inns**: resting, whether it's your hearthstone's inn, and how long until you're fully rested.

## Quiet when it should be

- Areas inside a zone get a smaller card, and riding back and forth over a border doesn't repeat them.
- Capital cities don't announce every street, and flight paths only show new zones (switch either on if you like).

## Make it yours

Esc → Options → AddOns → Landfall, or `/landfall`.

- **Classic**: Morpheus lettering and a gold divider.
- **Bronze**: the same on a bronze plaque that matches Forever's windows.
- **Cinematic**: thin black bars slide in behind your interface and the name appears in spaced ivory capitals in the lower third.
- **Minimal**: one small line.
- Short, normal or long; small, normal or large; drag it anywhere; every part can be switched off.
- Pick the sound for a new zone (swell, horn call, bells, harp), a discovery and danger, or none.
- A minimap button with a quick menu to change the look in one click.

## Commands

- `/landfall`: settings
- `/landfall test`: a card for where you are (`/landfall test sub`, `first`, `danger`)
- `/landfall menu`: the quick menu; `/landfall minimap` brings the button back
- `/landfall move`, `/landfall lock`, `/landfall reset`: place the card
- `/landfall on` / `/landfall off`

## Works with

WoW Forever and Classic Era. Lightweight: it only wakes up when you change zones.
```
